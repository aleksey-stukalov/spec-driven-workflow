# Execution policy

The extracted runtime uses `plans-v1`. Its [plan catalog](../plans.yaml) defines
supported profiles, requested routes, context targets and attempt allowances.
Follow the [execution procedure](../procedures/execute.md); package installation
does not launch workers or change a host's default model.

## Bounded work and independence

A run handles one selected change and only the applicable authorized stages.
Bind project, base revision, owned worktree, scope, authority, plan and evidence
before dispatch. Writers receive exclusive exact paths. Read-only assignments
use a retained source tree; command-based reproductions use separately assigned
scratch material.

The shipped catalog permits two concurrent workers per run and no nested delegation.
Coordinate slots across stages and providers. A shared ledger also accounts for
other runs using that ledger. Separate ledgers do not create a host-wide quota.
These are execution-profile limits, not a provider capacity claim. Host-local
coordination does not coordinate separate clones or machines.

Each design or implementation review round contains two independent lenses on
the same frozen inputs. Use fresh reviewer identities and contexts, distinct
from authors and other independent assignments. A reviewer may resume only its
same unfinished assignment against unchanged recorded inputs. Never accept an
author's self-review as independent acceptance.

Apply the selected plan's clean-round and per-step rules. Failed launches and
retries consume allowance; changing provider, restarting or selecting another
plan does not reset counters. Reserve enough later review capacity before a
repair. Missing capacity, incomplete coverage or unresolved serious findings
leave the phase incomplete.

## Settings and resources

Verify required model and effort availability, client options, conflicting
overrides and effective context capacity before launch. Record requested and
runtime-reported settings separately. Unknown settings or usage stay unknown.
Do not silently substitute a model, reduce effort or use a paid tier outside
the existing authority.

Context targets and concise result targets are working policies, not advertised
provider windows. Partition scope while preserving coverage when inputs do not
fit. Never truncate findings to obtain a clean result. Preserve complete
original reports separately from concise handoffs.

When a limit must increase, request one bounded extension that covers the
remaining repair, adjudication, review and verification. State used allowances,
the actual blocker and evidence, added allowances and resulting ceilings,
requested routes, estimates or unknown costs, and the stopping condition.
Reuse an approved extension within its scope; silence is not an extension.

## Evidence and recovery

Retain exact requests, prompts, complete original results, identities, timestamps,
source bundles, executable modes, deleted originals and available usage records
outside the worktree. Keep summaries separate. Bind check results and both review
phases to the relevant final source revisions.

Approved SDW discovery links are retained as inert target-text files with typed
metadata, alongside explicitly selected canonical skill content. Review both
the link identity and that content. Do not follow a retained record as a link or
omit old child-file originals displaced by a directory-to-link conversion.
Ordinary aliases remain forbidden; historical bundles keep their original schemas.

Missing, partial, stale, unbound or malformed reports do not count as clean.
Successful transport completion is not acceptance. Wait for launched siblings
to settle before dependent edits. An uncertain launch keeps its reservation
until recovery establishes its outcome; never blindly replay it.

Reconcile attempted writer edits and current source before retry or resume.
Preserve prior counters, evidence and remote operation receipts. A changed input
invalidates affected clean rounds; a new capture cannot impersonate the original
reviewed result. Review acceptance requires complete coverage, resolved defects
and required passing checks against the final bytes.

The validators verify supplied records and bindings. They do not prove review
depth, hidden context isolation, remote observation authenticity, production
behavior or model quality. Report the scope of actual verification.
